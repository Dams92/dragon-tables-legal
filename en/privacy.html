Dragon Tables – Privacy policy

Version

Privacy policy, version of 2026-11-26.

Data controller

Damien Lahitte, contact@wikifighter.app.

Data collected

Without an account: progress stays on the device; only the anonymous statistics described under “Usage statistics” are sent, unless a parent turns them off. With an account: the parent's email address, an encrypted password (or the Google or Apple identifier), the child's nickname, game progress and answer history (fact, right or wrong, answer time), and the date the terms were accepted.

Purposes

This data is only used to save progress, sync it between devices and adapt reviews to the facts that need practice. There are no ads, no commercial profiling and no data resale.

Usage statistics (optional)

On by default, and a parent can turn it off at any time in the settings (parent area): the app sends anonymous usage statistics to Google Firebase Analytics (screens opened, tables and activities played, successes, activities left, durations, game language), tied to a random identifier of the installation. No name, no email, no nickname, no advertising ID; ad personalization and data sharing for advertising are turned off. These statistics are only used to improve the game. Turning it off stops the measurement and erases the device's identifier. Google may process this data outside the European Union, with the safeguards provided by the GDPR.

Microphone (optional)

If “Answer with the microphone” is turned on, the child's voice is turned into a number by the phone's speech recognition, on the device when the phone allows it; otherwise Apple's or Google's speech service may be used. Dragon Tables neither keeps nor sends any recording: only the answer (the number) is kept, like a typed answer.

Legal basis

For the account: the consent of the parent or legal guardian, given when it is created. For usage statistics: the publisher's legitimate interest in improving the game; the parent can object at any time by turning them off in the settings.

Retention

Data is kept while the account exists. An account inactive for 2 years is deleted. Deleting the account erases all its data immediately. Usage statistics are kept for 14 months.

Recipients and hosting

Only the publisher can access the account data, which is hosted by Railway Corporation (https://railway.com) in the European Union. The anonymous usage statistics are processed by Google (Firebase Analytics) as a processor.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct, erase and port your data, and to object. From the app's settings you can download the account's data and delete the account. For any other request: contact@wikifighter.app. You may also contact the CNIL (www.cnil.fr).
