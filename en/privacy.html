Dragon Tables – Privacy policy

Version

Privacy policy, version of 2026-11-21.

Data controller

Damien Lahitte, contact@wikifighter.app.

Data collected

Without an account: no data leaves the device. With an account: the parent's email address, an encrypted password (or the Google or Apple identifier), the child's nickname, game progress and answer history (fact, right or wrong, answer time), and the date the terms were accepted.

Purposes

This data is only used to save progress, sync it between devices and adapt reviews to the facts that need practice. There are no ads, no commercial profiling, no third-party trackers and no data resale.

Microphone (optional)

If “Answer with the microphone” is turned on, the child's voice is turned into a number by the phone's speech recognition, on the device when the phone allows it; otherwise Apple's or Google's speech service may be used. Dragon Tables neither keeps nor sends any recording: only the answer (the number) is kept, like a typed answer.

Legal basis

The consent of the parent or legal guardian, given when the account is created.

Retention

Data is kept while the account exists. An account inactive for 2 years is deleted. Deleting the account erases all its data immediately.

Recipients and hosting

Only the publisher can access the data, which is hosted by Railway Corporation (https://railway.com) in the European Union.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct, erase and port your data, and to object. From the app's settings you can download the account's data and delete the account. For any other request: contact@wikifighter.app. You may also contact the CNIL (www.cnil.fr).
